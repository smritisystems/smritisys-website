Cookie Policy

Last updated: 20 August 2026

What cookies are

Cookies are small browser files. Similar technologies include local storage and session storage. They can help a website remember state, understand technical performance or support security.

Current public-site use

The public pages are primarily static. The navigation uses ordinary page state, and public forms or future measurement tools may use essential or optional technologies depending on the deployed configuration. The authenticated portal has separate session behavior governed by its own product context.

Managing cookies

You can delete or block cookies through your browser settings. Blocking essential storage may affect some website or portal behavior. Browser settings differ by device and browser.

Third-party services

If third-party analytics, video, forms or support services are enabled, their technologies may be governed by their own policies. Any consent or notice requirements should be reviewed for the actual deployment.

Contact

For questions about cookies or browser storage, use the contact form. This policy should be updated when the deployed public-site tooling changes.
