Answers for growing retail teams

Clear guidance for store owners, operators and expansion teams planning the next stage of growth.

Can I start with one store and expand later?

Yes. SMRITI is built to start small and scale to multiple stores, sales teams and online channels without changing systems.

Does it work for GST billing and compliance?

Yes. The platform supports GST-ready billing, invoice flows, item taxation and reporting patterns used by modern Indian retail businesses.

Does SMRITI replace the accounting system?

No. SMRITI manages the retail operations layer while the accounting and financial reporting layer remains with the finance system used by the business. Together they create a cleaner operating model for growth.

Can we connect our online store or marketplace?

Yes. You can launch your own storefront or connect your existing channels into one order and stock management workflow.

What does a pilot involve?

We review your stores, data, hardware and priority workflows, then agree the supported rollout scope and timeline together.
