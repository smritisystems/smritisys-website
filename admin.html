License inventory

Prepare activation codes

Generate up to 200 unassigned pairs with nine-digit numeric serials and 16-character alphanumeric activation keys. Keys are encrypted at rest and can be revealed one at a time after confirming your admin password. Older hash-only codes can be restored if you still have the original key. New licenses receive a 14-day offline lease followed by a 7-day grace period.

License authority

Issue a license

Issue an activation key without requiring a customer portal invite. The server registers the customer and organization, limits this license to one concurrent installation, and shows the activation key only once.

No customer sign-in or invitation is created. The customer receives only the serial number and activation key.
