Questions for evaluation

Shoper 9 alternative FAQ

What is a Shoper 9 alternative?

A Shoper 9 alternative is another retail software platform evaluated for POS, inventory, distribution, warehouse, reporting and implementation requirements.

Is SMRITI a replacement for Shoper 9?

SMRITI is an independent retail operations platform that can be evaluated as an alternative depending on requirements, edition and deployment scope. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Tally Solutions or Shoper 9.

Can SMRITI handle retail POS?

SMRITI is designed for retail POS, billing, checkout, sales and returns. Payment, printing and rollout scope vary by deployment.

Does SMRITI support distributor operations?

SMRITI is designed for retailer orders, wholesale sales, credit context, returns, purchase planning, dispatch and distribution workflows. Availability is deployment dependent.

Does SMRITI support warehouse management?

SMRITI is designed for warehouse and godown management, bulk dispatch, stock movement, transfers, in-transit visibility and physical stock take workflows. Confirm scope during a demo.

Does SMRITI support barcode printing?

Barcode Studio is a planned and deployment-dependent direction for barcode generation, label preparation, printing, stationery selection, print history and audit workflows.

Can I migrate from Shoper 9 to SMRITI?

Migration scope can be assessed during implementation after reviewing master data, transaction history, roles, warehouse structure, printers, barcode data and a pilot.

Does SMRITI support offline operations?

Offline local capture, queueing and later synchronization are planned and are not advertised as generally available.

Does SMRITI support multiple stores?

SMRITI is designed around branches, stores, transfers and multi-location contexts. Multi-store availability is deployment dependent.

Can I connect SMRITI with other systems?

Specific integrations and connectors should be reviewed and confirmed during demo and implementation scoping.
