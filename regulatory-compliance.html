Legal, risk & governance

Regulatory Compliance & Governance

A practical public overview for evaluating responsibilities around SMRITISYS and SMRITI Retail OS.

Compliance is contextual

Product capability does not replace business compliance advice.

Applicable obligations depend on the legal entity, location, industry, data, contracts, configuration, deployment and operating procedures. This page is general guidance, not legal, tax, accounting or regulatory advice. Obtain qualified advice for your circumstances.

REVIEW WITH COUNSEL

Privacy and personal data

Review the Privacy Policy, consent, notices, retention, access requests, processor terms and applicable Indian privacy requirements such as the DPDP framework.

DEPLOYMENT DEPENDENT

GST and tax workflows

GST, invoicing, e-invoicing, e-way bills, HSN, tax configuration and records require confirmation for the business, edition and implementation scope.

GOVERNANCE PRACTICE

Security and access

Use role-based access, least privilege, accountable administration, secure credentials, incident handling and appropriate review of integrations and hosting.

PUBLIC WEBSITE

Accessibility

The public website is designed with semantic headings, labels, keyboard-friendly controls and responsive layouts. Accessibility should be tested against the needs of each audience and deployment.

DEPLOYMENT DEPENDENT

Records and audit

Retention, transaction history, print history, stock records, approvals and audit trails should be defined with the business process and applicable retention obligations.

CONFIRM DURING DEMO

Certifications and assurance

No certification, audit report or regulatory approval should be inferred from this page. Request current evidence and contractual commitments during procurement.
