Founder story

From store-floor reality to enterprise systems thinking.

Jawahar R. Mallah built SMRITI around a core belief: retail businesses need systems that work in real environments, not just in boardroom presentations. The goal was to simplify operations without losing enterprise-level depth.

  • Hands-on understanding of multi-store retail challenges, inventory movement and sales execution.
  • Architecture built for operational clarity, branch visibility and workflow discipline.
  • Focus on simple systems that scale from single stores to larger retail networks.
