DEPLOYMENT DEPENDENT

Generation and assignment

Designed for item-linked barcode generation, SKU assignment, internal or custom codes where configured, duplicate detection and validation.

DEPLOYMENT DEPENDENT

Print sources and jobs

Print context may include Item Master, purchase, GRN, sales, returns, transfers, physical stock, batch, serial or direct scan workflows, with template, printer and quantity selection.

PLANNED / DEPLOYMENT DEPENDENT

Print governance

Designed to track job ID, user, time, source, item/SKU, batch/serial, requested/printed/failed quantity, printer, stationery, template, cancellation, reprint reason and final status.
