# SMRITI Saathi Asset Manifest

SMRITI Saathi is a modular visual communication system. The product remains the hero; characters explain the product.

## Selection Rule

Use this sequence before selecting an asset:

`CONTENT -> PURPOSE -> CHARACTER -> POSE/EXPRESSION -> ASSET`

Use the smallest appropriate asset that communicates the idea clearly. Prefer an individual male or female character for ordinary sections, cards, documentation, tutorials, onboarding and feature explanations. Use both characters together only when the communication benefits from showing the complete Saathi team.

## Context Mapping

| Content purpose | Preferred asset direction |
|---|---|
| Presentation title or major brand slide | `hero_` composition or `smriti_saathi_title_badge.png` |
| Product explanation | Individual `expression_*_explaining` or `pose_*_holding_laptop` |
| Feature explanation | Individual pointing or explaining pose |
| POS and billing | POS action asset, or explaining/pointing/tablet pose |
| Inventory | Inventory action asset, or folder/pointing pose |
| Customer management | Customer-support or welcoming asset |
| Targets and performance | Targets action asset or growth-chart pose |
| Success or completed action | Happy, excited or thumbs-up asset |
| Problem, decision or analysis | Thinking pose or expression |
| Welcome or onboarding | Happy, waving or welcoming pose |
| Support and help | Customer-support pose or explaining expression |

## Frozen Rules

- Do not invent filenames or create duplicate mascot styles.
- Search `assets/SmritiSaathi/` before requesting or creating a new asset.
- Do not stretch, crop, recolor or distort characters.
- Do not use a hero composition inside ordinary feature cards.
- Do not use the duo image everywhere; alternate male and female assets naturally.
- Keep sufficient whitespace around individual characters.
- Do not let the mascot overpower product UI, screenshots, feature information, architecture, pricing or CTAs.
