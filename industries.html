Industry-Ready Operating Workflows

Built Around the Way Your Retail Category Works

Every retail vertical has unique stock signals, billing nuances, and unit economics. SMRITI Retail OS adapts to your category without requiring brittle third-party plugins.

Footwear & Leather DEPLOYMENT DEPENDENT

Size Matrix & Single-Shoe Control

Handle the complexity of UK/US/EU size scales, colorways, box barcode generation, and broken-size markdown management.

✓ Multi-Dimensional Matrix: Size × Width × Color in a unified grid
✓ Box & Pair Barcoding: Custom thermal label printing directly from PO receipts
✓ Exchange & Mismatched Pair Handling: Built-in counter return validation
Apparel & Lifestyle DEPLOYMENT DEPENDENT

Collections, Fit & Fast Billing

Designed for high-traffic apparel counters with seasonal collections, style-level attributes, and automated mix-and-match promotional engines.

✓ Variant Generation: Instant SKU explosion across Style, Fit, and Fabric
✓ Promotion Engine: Buy 2 Get 1, Flat Off, and Tiered slab discount calculations
✓ Alteration Tracking: Store-to-tailor job slips and customer delivery notes
Jewellery & Precious Metals DEPLOYMENT DEPENDENT

UID Tracking & Wastage Calculations

Precise weight accounting, stone charge handling, dynamic daily metal rate linking, and government HUID compliance.

✓ Gross / Net / Stone Weight: Precise 3-decimal weight tracking at POS
✓ Daily Rate Board: Automatic price adjustments based on published gold/silver rates
✓ HUID & Hallmarking: Traceable unique piece identification for compliance
Supermarket & FMCG DEPLOYMENT DEPENDENT

Batch FIFO & Electronic Scale Sync

Engineered for sub-second scanning speed, weigh-scale integration, expiration tracking, and multi-pack loose item decomposition.

✓ Electronic Weighing Scale: RS-232 / USB scale integration with live weight capture
✓ Batch & Expiry FIFO: Automatic first-in-first-out stock depletion with near-expiry alerts
✓ Combo & Kit Items: Automatic parent-child stock breakdown at point of sale
Wholesale & Trade DEPLOYMENT DEPENDENT

Bulk Order Sheets, Credit Control & Dispatch

Bridge the gap between B2B trade orders and store replenishment with tiered volume price matrices, customer credit validation, and automated B2B e-invoice generation.

✓ Tiered Pricing: Customer group-specific price lists and volume discounts
✓ Credit Limits: Hard & soft credit stop rules on outstanding balances
✓ B2B E-Invoicing: Instant IRN and QR code generation for GST compliance

Industry workflows and availability vary by edition, deployment stage, and implementation scope.
