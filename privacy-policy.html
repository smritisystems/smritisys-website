Legal & privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 August 2026

Scope

This policy describes how information may be handled when you use the public SMRITISYS website, request a demo, contact the team or evaluate SMRITI Retail OS. Authenticated portal processing may be governed by additional customer agreements and deployment-specific notices.

Information we may receive

Depending on your interaction, this may include your name, work email, phone number, company, store or operating context, messages, and information you voluntarily provide in a product conversation. Standard technical information such as browser, device and request logs may also be processed to operate and secure the website.

How information is used

Information may be used to respond to enquiries, arrange demonstrations, understand requirements, provide support, improve the public website, protect services, maintain records and meet applicable legal obligations.

Sharing and retention

Information may be shared with service providers supporting hosting, communications, security or business operations, subject to appropriate controls. Retention depends on the purpose, relationship, legal requirements and operational need. We do not sell personal information.

Security and rights

Reasonable administrative, technical and organizational safeguards are used for the relevant processing context. Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, withdrawal of consent or information about processing. Contact us with enough detail to identify the request.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the contact form. This public policy is general information and should be reviewed with qualified counsel for your specific obligations.
